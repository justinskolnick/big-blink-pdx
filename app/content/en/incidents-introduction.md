Lobbying entities are required to register with the City Auditor and submit quarterly lobbying activity reports to the Auditor’s office ([2.12.040](https://www.portland.gov/code/2/12/040)). City Code identifies the information included in each report as public record ([2.12.100](https://www.portland.gov/code/2/12/100)).
